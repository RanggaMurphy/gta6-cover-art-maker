Neon Bay Cover Studio: turn your own photos into a multi-panel GTA-style poster cover, with a Vice City sunset and neon grade, bold editable lettering, and PNG export in 1080x1080, 1080x1350 and 1920x1080. Five poster layouts, five colour grades, per-panel crop with drag and zoom, film grain and vignette. Everything runs offline in a desktop browser from the static files under `app/`; photos never leave your machine. All artwork and fonts are original or freely licensed (Anton and Bebas Neue under the SIL Open Font License); no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font is used.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
